## Operators

**Arithmetic**
- `+` addition
- `-` subtraction
- `*` multiplication
- `/` division
- `%` modulo
- `**` exponentiation

**Comparison**
- `==` equal
- `!=` not equal
- `<` less than
- `>` greater than
- `<=` less than or equal
- `>=` greater than or equal

**Logical**
- `!` NOT
- `&&` logical AND
- `||` logical OR

**Bitwise**
- `&` AND
- `|` OR
- `^` XOR
- `<<` left shift
- `>>` right shift

**Assignment**
- `=` assign
- `:=` declare and assign
- `+=`, `-=`, `*=`, `/=`, etc. compound assignment

**Other**
- `--` / `++` decrement/increment respectively
- `...` spread
